title: Readable

order: 4
---
Make this site's main text comfortable to read, in the "readable" part.

- A measure of about 65–75 characters for body text (`max-width: 70ch` on the text column, centred), a slightly larger size where it's small (at least 17px for long text), and a line height around 1.5–1.65.
- Paragraph spacing, readable link colours with underlines in body text, images that fit the column.
- Reserve space for late-loading content so the page doesn't shift while you read (`aspect-ratio`, `min-height` on known slots).
- Only the reading column: leave navigation, toolbars and forms as they are.
